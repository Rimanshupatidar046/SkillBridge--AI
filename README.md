# SkillBridge AI

### From Industry Signals to Workforce Action

SkillBridge AI is an AI-powered workforce intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between **industry requirements, student skills, academic curriculum, training capacity, and employer feedback**.

Developed by **Team SkillPulse** for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**.

## 💡 Problem

Industry skill requirements are changing rapidly, while academic curricula, student capabilities, training resources, and employer expectations often remain disconnected.

This creates:

- Skill–industry mismatch
- Outdated or insufficient curriculum coverage
- Limited visibility of emerging skills
- Difficulty identifying student skill gaps
- Training capacity gaps
- Weak industry feedback loops

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

SkillBridge AI creates a connected workforce intelligence layer that analyzes industry demand, skills, proficiency, curriculum coverage, training capacity, and employer feedback.

The platform helps answer:

> **What skills does industry need?**  
> **What skills do students currently have?**  
> **Where are the skill and curriculum gaps?**  
> **What should be learned or changed?**  
> **Is sufficient training capacity available?**  
> **What does employer feedback tell us?**
