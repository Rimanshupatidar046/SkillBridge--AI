# SkillBridge AI

### From Industry Signals to Workforce Action

SkillBridge AI is an AI-powered workforce intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between **industry requirements, student skills, academic curriculum, and workforce development**.

Developed by **Team SkillPulse** for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**.

## 💡 Problem

Industry skill requirements are changing rapidly, while academic curricula, student capabilities, industry demand, and training ecosystems often remain disconnected.

This creates:

- Skill–Industry Mismatch
- Curriculum gaps
- Limited visibility of emerging skills
- Student skill gaps
- Difficulty in identifying priority skills
- Weak industry–education feedback loops

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

SkillBridge AI creates a connected workforce intelligence layer that links **industry demand, skills, proficiency, curriculum coverage, student capabilities, training capacity, and employer feedback**.

The platform helps answer:

> **What skills does industry need?**  
> **What skills do students currently have?**  
> **Where are the skill and curriculum gaps?**  
> **What should be learned or improved?**  
> **What workforce actions should institutions take?**

🧠 How SkillBridge AI Works

1. Industry Demand

Industry requirements and role-level skill needs are analyzed to understand workforce demand.

2. Skill Intelligence

Skills are mapped across industry roles, academic curricula, courses, and student profiles.

3. Proficiency Analysis

The platform considers not only whether a skill exists, but also the required and current proficiency level.

4. Gap Analysis

The system identifies the difference between required skills and current student/curriculum coverage.

5. Personalized Learning

Student-level gaps are converted into a structured learning pathway.

6. Workforce Action

Identified curriculum and training gaps can be converted into actionable interventions.

7. Employer Feedback

Employer and placement feedback can continuously improve future workforce intelligence.


---

📊 Gap Intelligence

SkillBridge AI distinguishes between multiple types of gaps.

Student Skill Gap

Required Skill Proficiency
          -
Current Student Proficiency

Curriculum Gap

Industry Skill Requirement
          -
Curriculum Skill Coverage

Training Gap

Required Training Capacity
          -
Available Training Capacity

This enables the platform to move beyond simply identifying a gap and toward actionable workforce planning.

🤖 Responsible AI Approach

SkillBridge AI separates AI-based understanding from deterministic application logic.

AI can assist with:

Skill extraction

Job description understanding

Curriculum text analysis

Semantic skill mapping

Gap explanations

Learning recommendations


Application logic handles:

Skill comparisons

Gap calculations

Priority rules

Scores

Thresholds

Analytics


This approach helps keep important calculations more controllable and explainable.
